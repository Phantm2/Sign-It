SDG GOAL 10 INEQUALITIES

HOW DID YOU HELP REDUCE INEQUALITY?

Imagine living in a world where communicating with others is difficult because people don't understand your language. This is a reality for many people who use sign language. By learning even a few sign-language gestures, you have taken a small step towards making communication more inclusive. This activity supports UN Sustainable Development Goal 10 – REDUCED INEQUALITIES – because it encourages equal opportunities, understanding, accessibility and inclusion for people with hearing and speech disabilities.

Remember: Inclusion begins when we make an effort to understand one another!
